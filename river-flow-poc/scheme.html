Water balance scheme

A major basin's water balance as a scheme on the map: each sub-basin's outflow to the next, its storage change and its rain and evapotranspiration — proof of concept. River flow · Rain · Evapotranspiration

Discharge Q

Along the rivers, from each sub-basin's dot through its outlet into the next sub-basin: the line's width is the mean outflow, so widths add up where rivers join. An arrow marks where the basin drains to the sea or a sink.

Storage change ΔS

On the river nearest the middle of each sub-basin: the dot's area is the mean change, green as storage rises, red as it falls.

Precipitation and evapotranspiration

Hover a sub-basin, line or dot for its balance: Q out = Q in + P − ET − ΔS. Model: FAO water balance on WaPOR v3 L1 PCP and AETI, per SDG 6 sub-basin and dekad, run in the browser. Outlets: the end of each sub-basin's biggest river (FAO rivers_world_47950); middles: from FAO's sub-basin tiles.
